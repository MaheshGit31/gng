## What he does
 
- **Replies to pings.** Ping him (or reply to one of his messages) and he answers in character.
- **Knows when you talk about him.** Say "gng" in a normal message, no ping needed, and he might respond.
- **Jumps into conversations.** He randomly chimes in on chats, with a cooldown per channel so he never spams.
- **Remembers the chat.** He keeps the last 15 messages of each channel, so his replies actually make sense.
- **Texts like a person.** Long answers get split into separate short messages with a small typing delay.
- **Sees images.** Post a picture and he reacts to what is actually in it.
